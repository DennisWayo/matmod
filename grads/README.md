# Graduate Track: Advanced Quantum Photonic Material Modeling

This folder is for graduate-level material, emphasizing research-grade methods and independent investigation.

## Focus

- Advanced quantum simulation and materials modeling
- Photonic material optimization for quantum chips
- Hydrogen storage/production and CO2 reduction research pipelines
- Reproducibility, benchmarking, and publication readiness

## Recommended Contents

- Advanced lecture modules and seminar notes
- Research problem statements and milestones
- Method comparison studies
- Manuscript outlines and thesis support artifacts

## Outcomes

Graduate students should be able to formulate original research questions, execute robust computational pipelines, and defend results in publication-quality formats.
